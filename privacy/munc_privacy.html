Privacy Policy

MUN Compass

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how MUN Compass ("the app"), developed by Kymion, handles your information. MUN Compass is a free app designed to help you organize Model UN conferences. It was built to work entirely on your device. It does not require an internet connection or an account, and it does not collect your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We do not have access to anything you enter into the app, and we do not receive any data about you or how you use it.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The information you enter, such as conference details, notes, and to-do lists, is saved only in the app's storage on your device. It is used solely to provide the app's features. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

4. Internet Access

MUN Compass works fully offline. The app does not send or receive data over the internet, and none of your information leaves your device through the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools, and it does not share data with any third parties.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, according to its own privacy policy. We have no access to that information.

6. Device Backups

Depending on your device settings, your phone's operating system may include app data in device backups, such as Android's backup to your Google account. These backups are managed by your device and your Google account, not by MUN Compass, and we cannot access them.

7. Data Security and Deletion

Because your information stays on your device, its security depends on how you protect your device, for example with a screen lock. You can delete all of the app's data at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in your device settings. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered by us, because we never had a copy.

8. Children's Privacy

MUN Compass is suitable for students of all ages taking part in Model UN. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the way the app handles information changes, for example if new features are added, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
